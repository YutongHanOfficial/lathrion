[02] Doctrine

How we
operate.

The standards every Lathrion company is held to.

  1. I

    Nothing is announced early.

    Products are disclosed when they work, not before. We prefer to be underestimated.

  2. II

    Accuracy is not negotiable.

    A confident wrong answer is the most dangerous thing software can produce. Where certainty is unavailable, we say so, or we publish nothing.

  3. III

    We own the stack.

    Data pipelines, models and interfaces are built in-house. Every figure we publish can be traced to its source.

  4. IV

    Free at the point of use.

    Most of what we build is free and requires no account. Reach comes first.

  5. V

    Independent brands.

    Each product carries its own name and earns its own reputation. The parent company remains in the background.

  6. VI

    A long horizon.

    Lathrion is built to become a conglomerate, one company at a time. Software is where it begins.

[03] The name

The name

λάθρα

láthraAdverbAncient Greek“Secretly, by stealth”

Pronunciation

LATH-ree-on

/ˈlæθ.ri.ɑn/

Lathrion is a coined name, built on λάθρα, Ancient Greek for “secretly.” The word comes from λανθάνω, “to escape notice,” the same root as λήθη, the forgetting of the river Lethe, and ἀλήθεια, truth: literally, the state of not being hidden.

Both meanings apply. The work is done out of view. What is published has to be true.
