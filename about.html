Portfolio

Lathrion owns and operates the following products. Each runs as an independent brand.

01

Data

Forecasting and public-data products

  • Ballot Desk Forecasts and live results for every race in the 2026 U.S. midterm elections, with historical results back to 1976. Electionsballotdesk.org ↗
  • Six Degrees A searchable graph of documented connections between more than seven million people, built from public data. Knowledge graph ↗
02

Games

Daily geography and knowledge games

  • Meridian A geography game played on a 3D globe, with five modes and a daily challenge. Geography ↗
  • Echolocate A daily game in which players find a hidden city using distance clues. Geography ↗
  • Sift A daily puzzle in which players pick and rank the five correct answers out of eight. Trivia ↗
  • Zip Drop A geography game in which players place U.S. ZIP codes on a satellite map. Geography ↗
03

Media

Consumer film products

  • Movie Ratings A platform for rating, reviewing and discussing films. Filmmovieratings.me ↗

Principles

How we build and run our products.

  1. 01

    Accuracy

    We publish only what the data supports, and we state uncertainty plainly.

  2. 02

    Ownership

    We build our own data pipelines, models and software, so we can account for every result we publish.

  3. 03

    Discretion

    We announce products when they are ready, not before.

  4. 04

    Accessibility

    Most of our products are free to use and do not require an account.

  5. 05

    Independence

    Each product operates under its own brand and is judged on its own record.

  6. 06

    Long-term focus

    We are building Lathrion over decades, one company at a time.

About

Lathrion was founded by Yutong Han as Yutong Studios. It is a holding company for a growing set of software products, and it intends to expand into further sectors over time.

The name comes from the Ancient Greek λάθρα (láthra), meaning “in secret.”
